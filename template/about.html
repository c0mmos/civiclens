Our story

Technology that helps communities respond faster.

CivicLens was created to make civic reporting more visible, accountable, and easier for everyone involved. Too often, residents face long delays or unclear communication when they report problems in their neighborhood.

We built a platform that connects everyday community observations with city service workflows so residents can report issues quickly and staff can respond with greater clarity and accountability.

“If residents can see what has been reported, what is in progress, and what has been resolved, city services become more accountable and more trusted.”
